# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good faith, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@rokib16x](https://github.com/rokib16x)). Reports are handled privately.
